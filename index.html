What we do

Building services for your home

From a single room to a full renovation, we handle the building work and coordinate the trades, so you have one team to deal with.

Extensions

Single and double-storey, rear and side-return extensions that give you more space without the move.

Loft conversions

Turn unused roof space into a bedroom, office or ensuite, with dormers and roof windows to suit.

Renovations & refurbishments

Internal alterations, knock-throughs, re-plastering and complete refurbishments of older properties.

Kitchens & bathrooms

Full strip-out and refit, including the building work, plumbing and electrics coordination and finishing.

Driveways, patios & groundworks

Block paving, patios, drainage, foundations and garden walls, built to last.

Repairs & maintenance

Brickwork, repointing, roofing repairs and the jobs that need doing properly, however small.
